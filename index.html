How these are calculated

Individual trophy probabilities come directly from Kalshi prediction market mid-prices (average of best bid and best ask, in cents). Multi-trophy and Quad probabilities treat each competition as independent and multiply individual probabilities. This is a simplification — in reality, squad fatigue, fixture congestion, and momentum create correlations. The "at least N trophies" figures use inclusion-exclusion over all C(4,k) combinations.
