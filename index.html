How these are calculated

Individual probabilities come from Kalshi market mid-prices (avg of best bid and ask, in cents). Multi-trophy odds treat each competition as independent. The "at least N" figures use inclusion-exclusion over all C(4,k) combinations. This is a simplification — fatigue and fixture congestion create real correlations.
